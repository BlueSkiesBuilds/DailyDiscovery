Drive Sync

Your profile is saved locally on this device. Use Drive sync to share it across devices. Both Push and Pull use the same merge logic - sources and scores are combined, taking the stronger value where they differ.

Pull from Drive

Download the Drive master and merge into your local profile. Adds sources and ratings from other devices.

Push to Drive

Merge your local profile with the Drive master and save the result back. Updates the master for other devices.

How to use across devices

1. Use the app on Device A, rating articles to build your profile.

2. Push from Device A to save to Drive.

3. On Device B, Pull from Drive to import that profile.

4. Both devices continue independently. Push or Pull again whenever you want to consolidate.
